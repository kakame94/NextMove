How it works

SMS comes in. Klaris qualifies. You close.

Three steps. No form to fill. No app to learn.

1

Prospect texts your number

Your usual phone number receives the SMS. Klaris intercepts before it lands in your notifications.

2

Klaris asks the right questions

Buying or selling? Area? Budget? Pre-qualified? Timeline? Five questions, one clean summary.

3

Hot lead in your dashboard

Notification + full record + temperature score. You call when timing's right. Klaris follows up if needed.

Features

Built with brokers, for brokers.

Not a generic CRM. Klaris speaks your vocabulary — Centris, OACIQ, mortgage, plex.

SMS qualification chatbot

Five structured questions per buyer or seller flow. Warm, natural Quebecois tone. Signed "his/her assistant" — never disguised AI.

CRM with temperature scoring

Five levels, from cold (blue) to scorching (red). Calculated from answers, timing and mortgage pre-qualification.

Smart auto follow-ups

If the prospect doesn't reply, Klaris follows up — under rules you approved. Business hours, QC holidays, sender score, all respected.

Bilingual EN/FR — auto-detect

Klaris detects the language of the first message and continues there. No setting to toggle. Anglo and franco prospects, same flows.
